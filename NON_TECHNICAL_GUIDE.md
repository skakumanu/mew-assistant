# 📅 View Your Google Calendar (Non-Technical Guide)

## What You Need
- A computer or phone with internet
- A Google account
- 2 minutes

---

## Step 1: Get the Calendar Viewer

**Click this link and save the file:**
https://raw.githubusercontent.com/skakumanu/mew-assistant/feature/customerzerosetup/calendar-simple.html

**How to save:**
- **On computer:** Right-click the page → "Save As" → Save to Desktop
- **On phone:** Tap the download icon or "Download" button

**File name:** `calendar-simple.html`

---

## Step 2: Open the File

**On computer:**
- Find the file on your Desktop
- Double-click it
- It opens in your web browser (Chrome, Safari, etc.)

**On phone:**
- Open your "Downloads" or "Files" app
- Find `calendar-simple.html`
- Tap it to open

---

## Step 3: Connect Your Calendar

You'll see a big blue button that says:

**"🔐 Connect Google Calendar"**

**Click it!**

Then:
1. Choose your Google account
2. Click "Allow" when Google asks for permission
3. You'll come back to the calendar page

---

## Step 4: See Your Calendar

Now you'll see a big blue button that says:

**"📅 Show My Events"**

**Click it!**

Your calendar events will appear! 🎉

---

## That's It!

You can close the page and open it again anytime.

Your calendar stays connected for 30 days.

## Common Questions

**Q: Do I need to download an app?**
A: No! Just open the HTML file in any web browser.

**Q: Is this safe?**
A: Yes! It only reads your calendar. Can't change or delete anything.

**Q: Do I pay anything?**
A: Nope! Completely free.

**Q: What if I close the page?**
A: Just open the file again. You're still connected!

**Q: How do I disconnect?**
A: Close the file and delete it. That's it!

**Q: It says "Please sign in again"?**
A: Just click "Connect Google Calendar" again. Takes 30 seconds.

## Need Help?

**Can't find the file after downloading?**
- Look in your "Downloads" folder
- Or search your computer for "calendar-simple.html"

**Button doesn't work?**
- Make sure you have internet connection
- Try refreshing the page
- If still stuck, download the file again

**Don't see your events?**
- Make sure you clicked "Allow" when Google asked
- Try clicking "Show My Events" again
- If that doesn't work, start over from Step 3
