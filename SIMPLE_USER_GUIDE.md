## 🛠️ Troubleshooting

### "Please sign in first"
**What happened:** Your session expired (happens after 30 days)  
**Fix:** Just click "Sign in with Google" again

### Calendar shows "Loading..." forever
**What happened:** Connection issue  
**Fix:** 
1. Refresh the page (press F5)
2. Click "Show My Events" again
3. If still loading, sign out and sign in again

### No events showing
**What happened:** Either you have no upcoming events, or permission wasn't granted  
**Fix:**
1. Check your Google Calendar to confirm you have events
2. Sign out and sign in again, making sure to click "Allow" when Google asks

### Button doesn't work
**What happened:** Temporary glitch  
**Fix:**
1. Refresh the page (press F5)
2. Clear your browser cache
3. Try a different browser
