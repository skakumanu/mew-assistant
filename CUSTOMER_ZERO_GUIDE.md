# 🎯 Customer Zero Guide - View Your Google Calendar

## ⚡ 2 Steps, 2 Minutes Total

---

## Step 1: Add Yourself as Test User (One Time)

**Go to:** https://console.cloud.google.com/apis/credentials/consent

1. Scroll down to **"Test users"** section
2. Click **"+ ADD USERS"**
3. Type your email address
4. Click **"SAVE"**

✅ Done! This is a one-time setup.

---

## Step 2: View Your Calendar

**Go to:** https://mew-assistant-dev.gentlehill-b3306295.westus2.azurecontainerapps.io/calendar

1. Click **"🔐 Sign in with Google"**
2. Choose your Google account
3. Click **"Allow"** for calendar permission
4. You're automatically redirected back
5. Click **"📅 Show My Events"**
6. **Your calendar appears!** 🎉

## Troubleshooting

**Q: I get "Error 403: access_denied"**
A: Go back to Step 1 and add yourself as a test user

**Q: It says "Your session expired"**
A: This is fixed! Just refresh the page after signing in.

**Q: Don't see my events?**
A: Click "Show My Events" button. Make sure you have events in your Google Calendar.

**Q: Signed out accidentally?**
A: Just click "Sign in with Google" again. Takes 10 seconds.
