# What's new in Mew

Plain-language notes about what changed, written for a parent using the
app - not for a developer reading a diff. For the full engineering detail
behind each release, see [`CHANGELOG.md`](../CHANGELOG.md).

---

## August 24, 2026

- Your child, your care team, and you can now all see the same schedule.
  A change your child or their therapist asks for happens right away if
  it fits the rules you've already set - no back-and-forth needed. If it
  doesn't fit, you get one simple choice to make, with a few good
  alternative times already suggested.
- Calendars stay in sync automatically. If you or your care team use
  Google Calendar (or another calendar app), a schedule change made in
  Mew shows up there too.
- You'll get a message about anything that needed your decision, and a
  quiet, readable list of everything that was handled for you without
  needing to ask.
- Mew now speaks Spanish, Hindi and Arabic in addition to English (the
  Hindi and Arabic wording is still being reviewed by a native speaker
  before we'd call it fully ready).
- Lots of small accessibility improvements: bigger buttons, calmer
  motion, and information that's never shown using color alone.

## September 2026

- Nothing new for you to see this time - this update was behind-the-
  scenes housekeeping (how we track version numbers, catch accidental
  secrets, and keep our own documentation honest) so future updates stay
  reliable.
