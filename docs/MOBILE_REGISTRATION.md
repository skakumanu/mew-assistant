# 📱 Mobile Registration Guide

## For Parents - Register in 30 Seconds

### From Your iPhone

1. **Open Safari** and visit:
   ```
   https://mew-assistant-dev.gentlehill-b3306295.westus2.azurecontainerapps.io/
   ```

2. **Tap "Sign in with Google"** or **"Sign in with Apple"**

3. **Authorize** when prompted

4. **Done!** You're now registered

### From Your Android Phone

1. **Open Chrome** and visit:
   ```
   https://mew-assistant-dev.gentlehill-b3306295.westus2.azurecontainerapps.io/
   ```

2. **Tap "Sign in with Google"**

3. **Authorize** when prompted

4. **Done!** You're now registered

## What Happens Next?

After registration, you'll automatically:
- ✅ Be logged in
- ✅ Have a parent account created
- ✅ See your dashboard with options to:
  - Connect your Google/Apple Calendar
  - Add your phone number for SMS
  - Set up voice commands (Siri/Google Assistant)
  - Add family members (kids, caregivers)

---

## Next Steps

### 1. Connect Your Calendar

**iPhone (Apple Calendar):**
- Tap "Connect Calendar" → "Apple Calendar"
- Sign in with your Apple ID
- Grant calendar access

**Android (Google Calendar):**
- Tap "Connect Calendar" → "Google Calendar"
- Already connected! (uses your Google account)

### 2. Enable Voice Commands

**iPhone (Siri):**
- Scan the QR code shown on dashboard
- Or visit: https://mew-assistant-dev.gentlehill-b3306295.westus2.azurecontainerapps.io/shortcuts/siri

**Android (Google Assistant):**
- Say "Hey Google, talk to Mew Assistant"
- Follow the linking instructions

### 3. Add Your Phone Number

- Tap "Settings" → "Notifications"
- Enter your phone number
- Verify with the code sent via SMS
- Choose notification preferences (SMS/WhatsApp)

## For Kids (with Parent Approval)

Kids can also register, but they need parent approval:

1. **Kid** signs in using their own Google/Apple account
2. **Kid** selects "I'm a kid" during registration
3. **Parent** receives approval request
4. **Parent** approves and links kid to their account
5. **Kid** can now make schedule suggestions (requires parent approval)

## Troubleshooting

### "OAuth Error" or "Authorization Failed"
- Try clearing your browser cache
- Make sure you're using the latest Safari/Chrome
- Try incognito/private mode

### "Can't Connect Calendar"
- Make sure you granted calendar permissions
- Check Settings → Privacy → Calendar (iPhone)
- Check Google Calendar permissions (Android)

### "Voice Commands Not Working"
- Make sure you installed the Siri Shortcut (iPhone)
- Check that Google Assistant is linked (Android)
- Verify your account in the app
