## 🎭 Smart Approval System

Parents don't get overwhelmed:

### Auto-Approved (Low Risk):
- "What's my schedule today?"
- "When is my next appointment?"
- Requests within approved time windows
- Routine activities already scheduled

### Quick Approval (Medium Risk):
- Schedule changes within same day
- Adding activities to free time slots
- Standard appointment types

### Required Approval (High Risk):
- Canceling important appointments
- Scheduling during school/therapy
- Adding new types of activities
- Anything outside normal patterns

### Batch Approvals:
- Multiple requests → One notification
- "Alex wants 3 things, approve all?"
- Smart grouping by context
