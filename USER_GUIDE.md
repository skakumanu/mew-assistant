## 🚀 Quick Start (3 Minutes)

### Step 1: Sign In with Google

1. **Open this link on any device:**
   
   👉 https://mew-assistant-dev.gentlehill-b3306295.westus2.azurecontainerapps.io/calendar

2. **Click "Sign in with Google"**

3. **Choose your Google account**

4. **Click "Allow" when asked for calendar permission**
   - Mew Assistant needs to read your calendar
   - Your data stays private and secure

5. **Done!** ✅ You're signed in for 30 days

## 📅 View Your Calendar

### In a Web Browser

**After signing in:**
1. You'll see: "Welcome back, [Your Name]!"
2. Click **"Show My Events"**
3. Your upcoming calendar events appear instantly! 📅

**Bookmark it!** You can return anytime - no need to sign in again (for 30 days)

### On Your iPhone

**Same as above, works in Safari!**
1. Open the link in Safari
2. Sign in once
3. Add to Home Screen (optional):
   - Tap Share button
   - Tap "Add to Home Screen"
   - Now it's like a real app! 📱

## 🔐 Security & Privacy

### What We Access
- ✅ Your name and email (to sign you in)
- ✅ Your Google Calendar events (read-only)
- ❌ We **cannot** add or modify events (yet)
- ❌ We **cannot** access other Google services

### How Your Data is Protected
- 🔒 All connections use HTTPS encryption
- 🔒 Tokens stored securely in encrypted database
- 🔒 Your calendar data never leaves the secure connection
- 🔒 You can revoke access anytime

### To Revoke Access
1. Go to: https://myaccount.google.com/permissions
2. Find "Mew Assistant"
3. Click "Remove Access"

## 🆘 Troubleshooting

### "Error 403: access_denied"
**Problem:** You're not added as a test user yet

**Solution:**
1. Ask the admin to add you as a test user
2. Or wait for the app to be published (coming soon)

### "Please sign in again"
**Problem:** Your 30-day session expired

**Solution:** Just click "Sign in with Google" again - takes 5 seconds!

### "No events found"
**Possible reasons:**
- You have no upcoming events in Google Calendar
- Your calendar is empty
- The calendar permission wasn't granted

**Try:**
1. Add a test event in Google Calendar
2. Return to Mew Assistant
3. Click "Show My Events" again

### Calendar not loading?
1. Check your internet connection
2. Refresh the page (F5)
3. Try signing out and back in
